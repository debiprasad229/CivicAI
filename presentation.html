Problem Statement

The Critical Breakdown in Urban Civic Maintenance

Cities struggle to remediate hazardous infrastructure rapidly due to disconnected systems.

⏱️
Reporting Inertia

Citizens find bureaucratic municipal helplines opaque, discouraging timely reporting of localized road craters, water leaks, and streetlight outages.

📑
Manual Triage Fatigue

Municipal control rooms receive thousands of unstructured tickets weekly. Staff manually classify departments, creating multi-day bottlenecks.

🔁
Ticket Bloat & Duplicates

A single water pipeline rupture triggers 50+ individual reports from the same street. Without spatial deduplication, field crews are dispatched redundantly.

📉
No Action Roadmaps

Engineers receive vague citizen text without safety protocols, equipment lists, or risk prioritization, slowing down emergency resolution.

CivicAI System Architecture 03 / 06
AI & GIS Innovation

Intelligent Triage & Spatial Hotspot Clustering

Cutting-edge multimodal reasoning powered by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash.

🤖 Google Gemini 2.5 Multimodal Analysis

Every incoming grievance is passed to the Gemini 2.5 Flash SDK orchestrator with structured JSON schema guarantees:

• Severity Quantification: CRITICAL / HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW (0–100 index)
• Affected Demographic: Detects School Children, Hospital routes, Pedestrians
• Field Action Plan: "Dispatch vacuum de-watering pump, isolate water main valve"
• Standardized Summary: Auto-translates regional inputs into unified English dossier
🗺️ Geoapify GIS & Radar Hotspot Heatmap

Spatial intelligence clusters proximate complaints to identify systemic infrastructure failures:

• Radius Aggregation: Detects multi-complaint zones within 150m–300m
• Pulsating Visualizer: Radar wave animations highlight high-hazard clusters
• Severity Weighting: Critical hazards elevate the entire zone priority score
• Duplicate Suppression: Prevents issuing duplicate contractor work orders

Production Ready

Enterprise Tech Stack & Zero-Leak Security

Engineered for high availability, low latency (<200ms), and strict access control.

Frontend

React 19 & Vite: Lightning-fast SPA with lazy code-splitting.

Leaflet GIS & Recharts: Vector maps and responsive analytics.

Backend API

Node.js & Express: Modular ES Modules architecture.

Serverless on Vercel: Auto-scaling microservices with sub-second cold starts.

OWASP Hardening: Strict CORS, rate limiting, and helmet headers.

Database & AI

MongoDB Atlas: Geospatial 2dsphere indexing for instant proximity queries.

Google Gemini SDK: Autonomous reasoning via `@google/genai` 2.5 Flash.

Geoapify API: High-precision geocoding and reverse lookup.

Security & RBAC

Zero Secret Leakage: Gemini API keys & MongoDB URI strictly server-side.

JWT + bcryptjs: Stateless authentication with citizen vs admin route guards.

Audit Timeline: Immutable history for every municipal action.
